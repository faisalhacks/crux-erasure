## Paragraph for related work (all PENDING VERIFICATION)

**Bridging and consensus selection.** Bridging-based ranking, as used in Community Notes [CITE:wojcik2022] and surveyed as "bridging systems" [CITE:ovadya2023], promotes items endorsed across groups that usually disagree (PENDING VERIFICATION). Two studies warn that such selection can disfavour contested content. A naive bridging ranker downranked URLs on controversial topics in 70 million Facebook shares [CITE:milli2024], and ranking comments for conversational quality may censor certain groups [CITE:revel2025]. A 2026 comparison of opinion-selection algorithms likewise cautions that consensus-seeking selection can "flatten conflicting preferences" and erase minority voices [CITE:hafid2026]. Generative social choice [CITE:fish2023] counters this by returning a proportionally representative slate and measuring felt representation. These works study items, comments or slates, not the choice among candidate group statements by participants' own rankings, which is our test. We found no study of that choice (our search was keyword-based, and we read only abstracts or partial text for most of these papers).
